export const metadata = { title: "Check a policy against a standard" }

Use DocuSense to check an internal policy against the standard it's meant to follow, and get a list of
every place they disagree, with both sides quoted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add both documents">
    ```bash
    docusense add policies/mail-policy.md
    docusense add standards/rfc5321.pdf --doc smtp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for an audit">
    In your agent:

    > Audit the mail policy against the SMTP standard. For each conflict, quote both sides with citations
    > and tell me whether it looks like a real problem, a deliberate exception, or a false alarm.

    Or pick the built-in **Audit for contradictions** prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the results">
    You'll get a list like this:

    | Policy says | Standard says |
    |---|---|
    | Return undeliverable mail after **2 days** | Give up after at least **4-5 days** |
    | Retry after **15 minutes** | Retry interval at least **30 minutes** |
    | **Must not** support VRFY | **Should** support VRFY |

    Decide what to change. DocuSense never makes that call for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the fixes">
    After the policy is updated, add it again and ask:

    > Which conflicts with the SMTP standard were fixed in the new mail policy?
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- **Pull every rule on a topic** from both documents: "List every requirement about retries in the
  policy and in the standard."
- **Check definitions first.** Many apparent conflicts are two documents using the same word differently:
  "How does each document define 'relay server'?"
- **Want fewer false alarms?** Run `docusense conflicts --llm-judge` to have an AI model review each match.
  See [Settings](/docs/reference/settings#ai-review).
